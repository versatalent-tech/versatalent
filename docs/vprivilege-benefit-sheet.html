The offer in one paragraph

V•PRIVILEGE is about access, not money off. Members get a numbered Founding card, priority entry, first access to tickets, a welcome drink and a birthday drink, two guest passes, four members-only nights and a chance at artist experiences. It is sold separately from the free Silver, Gold and Black tiers. Paying does not raise a member's tier or bar discount, and a member who lets it lapse keeps their tier and points.

Members pay once for 12 months. Nothing renews automatically: 30 days before expiry the member gets a reminder and can buy another year from their pass page or at the bar.

Benefits are managed in the admin. The list below is the launch configuration, not a fixed menu. Admins can add, edit, pause or retire benefits and change their limits, cost and eligibility at any time. One rule protects members and the business: a change applies to memberships bought after it. Anyone who has already paid keeps the benefits they were sold until their 12 months end, unless the terms allow otherwise.

What the membership does not include

  • No tier. Gold and Black are still earned by attending and spending.
  • No extra bar discount. The member's earned tier discount applies as usual.
  • No status points. Welcome and birthday drinks use the reward balance.
  • No automatic renewal. It is a one-off payment for 12 months; members get a reminder 30 days before expiry and choose whether to buy another year.

For the membership terms

  • Price, the 12-month period and the full benefit list, shown before payment.
  • The 14-day right to cancel online purchases, and how benefits already used are deducted. Confirm the wording with an adviser.
  • Every benefit is subject to capacity and the limits above.
  • How benefit changes work: VersaTalent may change the benefit list for future purchases; benefits already sold are honoured for the paid 12 months. Confirm the wording with an adviser.
  • One-off payment: no card details are kept and nothing is charged again.
  • What happens at expiry: paid benefits stop, while tier and points stay.
  • Age limits on alcohol benefits.
